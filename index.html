TrueVolume™ Assesses Four Key Pillars

Locked terminology — the only terms used in consumer-facing outputs

Every player evaluation runs through the same four mechanical checks. The system is transparent — you can see exactly where we stand on each one.

01 — Infrastructure

Team Setup

Is the environment built to support this player's output? O-line stability, scheme fit, coaching philosophy, supporting cast.

02 — Opportunity

Volume Reality

How much offensive volume actually exists? A player can't accumulate stats on the sideline.

03 — Narrative Check

Hype Filter

Is the reasoning causal or recycled narrative? If the same argument works with the names swapped, it's a trap.

04 — Deployment

Real Role

What the player actually does on the field — snap share by package, route participation — not the depth chart title.

Output: Verdict (BUY / HOLD / FADE / TRAP), floor, ceiling, and the mechanical driver that created it.
